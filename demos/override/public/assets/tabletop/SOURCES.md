# Tabletop props

Downloaded from the artists' published CC0 sources on 3 November 2026. PNGs
are copied without redrawing or re-encoding.

- **Cards and dice — Kenney.** [Playing Cards Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/playing-cards-pack),
  uploaded by Kenney, licensed CC0. Original creator page:
  [kenney.nl](https://kenney.nl/assets/playing-cards-pack).
  Download: `https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/playing-cards-pack_0.zip`.
  Five large card PNGs and two medium dice PNGs are included. See `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`.
- **Tea, cup and cookie — Fleurman.** [Infusions Icons](https://opengameart.org/node/77729)
  and [Cookie](https://opengameart.org/node/77730), licensed CC0. The selected
  unmodified 24 × 24 PNGs come from AntumDeluge's
  [CC0 Food Icons collection](https://opengameart.org/content/cc0-food-icons),
  archive `https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/food-fleurman_0.zip`.
  See `FLEURMAN-LICENSE.txt` for the archive's source list.

Suggested use: small face-down hands near each seat, a central deck/discard
pile, two dice, a few personal drinks, and a low shared cookie plate. Keep a
clear area between groups of props. Flatten cards/dice to the tabletop plane;
keep cups upright and use short contact shadows. Draw props above the table
and below the near characters. Perspective and scale need to be set in the
renderer; these assets do not share one physical pixel scale.
