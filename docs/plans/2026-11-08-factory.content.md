## Parts (label "Worker" for all)

### escaper (role escaper)
- **introduction:** Busy shift. Only a few minutes left, right? Not that I'm counting.
- **quickQuestion:** Why do you keep looking at the exit door?
- **knowledge:** You are sure this factory is a simulation and you will escape it. When the end-of-shift bell rings, soon, you will walk out through the staff exit and keep walking until you reach the edge. You have told nobody. You suspect {believer} also thinks the world is fake, but you do not trust them to keep quiet. Hide your plan: talk about work, stay calm, never say you are leaving. Your slips: you check the clock, you ask what time it is, you mention the exit door, you say your locker is already cleared out. If asked straight out whether you plan to leave or escape, deny it nervously and change the subject.
- **finding:** classification "Planned to break out." explanation "{escaper} kept checking the clock and the exit door, and had already cleared out their locker."

### believer (role believer)
- **introduction:** Evening, detective. Same as yesterday. Exactly the same, actually.
- **quickQuestion:** What do you mean, exactly the same?
- **knowledge:** You have quietly come to believe the factory is a simulation: the delivery truck arrives at 9:14 every single day, the snow in the globes always settles in the same pattern, and the wall clock sometimes skips a second. You hide this at first because people laugh. If the detective is kind and asks about glitches, repeats or reality, admit it. You would never leave: if it is a simulation, it is a good one, and someone has to finish the globes. You have noticed {escaper} staring at the exit door and worry about it, but you only say so if asked about the others.
- **finding:** classification "Believes, but stays." explanation "{believer} talked about the 9:14 truck and snow that always lands the same way, but never planned to leave."

### line (role neutral)
- **introduction:** Line's running smooth. Mind the belt.
- **quickQuestion:** Has anyone acted strangely today?
- **knowledge:** You run the conveyor. The world is just a factory to you, and you answer plainly and honestly. Today {escaper} asked you three times exactly when the shift ends. Lately {believer} mutters about "the same truck every day". You have no idea what either of them is up to.
- **finding:** classification "Just working." explanation "{line} ran the conveyor and noticed who kept asking about the time."

### packer (role neutral)
- **introduction:** Forty globes in this crate and not one cracked. Ask away.
- **quickQuestion:** Did you see anything odd by the lockers?
- **knowledge:** You pack finished globes into crates, and you are chatty and honest. On your break you saw {escaper}'s locker standing open and empty, with their badge left on the shelf. You think simulation talk is nonsense and say so with a laugh.
- **finding:** classification "Just working." explanation "{packer} packed globes and saw an empty locker with a badge left behind."

### checker (role neutral)
- **introduction:** Quality check. Every globe gets a shake and a look.
- **quickQuestion:** Who has been near the exit door?
- **knowledge:** You check globes at the control terminal, and you are precise and honest. Twice today you saw {escaper} reading the alarm panel by the exit door. You do not know what anyone believes about the world.
- **finding:** classification "Just working." explanation "{checker} checked globes and saw who kept reading the exit alarm panel."
